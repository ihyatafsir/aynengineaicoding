# STEP-BY-STEP PROVISIONAL PATENT FILING GUIDE
**SYSTEM:** USPTO Patent Center (Recognized worldwide under the Paris Convention)

---

##  Step 1: Access the USPTO Patent Center
1. Open your browser and navigate to: **https://patentcenter.uspto.gov/**
2. Click on **"File a New Application"** (No special attorney account required for independent inventors; you can file as a "Guest / Unregistered User" or create a free USPTO.gov account).

---

##  Step 2: Select Application Type
1. Select **"Provisional Application for Patent (35 U.S.C. 111(b))"**.
2. Fill in the Inventor Details:
   - **Inventor Name:** [Your Full Legal Name]
   - **Residence / Country:** Switzerland (or your country of residence)
   - **Title of Invention:**
     `SYSTEM AND METHOD FOR HIERARCHICAL SYMBOLIC-NEURAL MIXTURE OF EXPERTS (H-MoE) SPECULATIVE INFERENCE AND EPISTEMIC CODE SYNTHESIS`

##  Step 4: Claim Micro Entity & Pay Filing Fee
1. Check the box for **"Micro Entity Status"** (Certification under 37 CFR 1.29).
2. The government filing fee is **~$60 to $140 USD**.
3. Pay via credit card.

---

##  Step 5: Instant Serial Number & Priority Date
- The moment payment is processed, the USPTO provides an immediate **Filing Receipt** containing:
  - **US Provisional Application Number (e.g., US 63/xxx,xxx)**
  - **Official Filing Date & Timestamp**
- Your invention is now legally **"Patent Pending" across 177 countries worldwide** for the next 12 months!
